Executive Summary

Project Overview

The Hotel Booking Analytics Dashboard provides an integrated business intelligence solution for analyzing hotel booking demand, customer behavior, revenue performance, and statistical insights. The platform combines interactive visualizations, statistical analysis, and data exploration to support informed business decisions and improve operational efficiency.

Key Business Highlights
  • Comprehensive analysis of booking, customer and revenue performance.
  • Statistical validation using hypothesis testing, correlation and confidence intervals.
  • Interactive dashboard for business monitoring and reporting.
  • Advanced data exploration with search, filtering and export functionality.
  • Professional reporting platform for management decision support.
