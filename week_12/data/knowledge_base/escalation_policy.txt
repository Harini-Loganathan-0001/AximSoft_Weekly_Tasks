Customer Support Escalation Policy

Customer issues should be escalated to human support when the available information is insufficient to provide a reliable answer.

Billing disputes involving incorrect charges, duplicate charges, or transactions that cannot be verified should be reviewed by the billing support team.

Refund requests should be escalated when refund eligibility cannot be determined from the available purchase information and applicable refund conditions.

Cancellation requests should be escalated when the current order status does not show whether cancellation is still possible.

Technical issues should be escalated when documented troubleshooting steps are unavailable, unsuccessful, or inappropriate for the reported problem.

Technical issues involving possible safety concerns should not be handled through unsupported troubleshooting instructions and should be referred to appropriate human support.

Warranty requests should be escalated when product information, purchase information, warranty conditions, or eligibility cannot be verified.

Delivery issues should be escalated when an order is marked as delivered but the customer has not received it and the available delivery information cannot resolve the issue.

Account-related problems should be escalated when the available customer information is insufficient to verify or resolve the issue.

Payment problems should be escalated when the transaction status cannot be verified or when repeated payment attempts may create additional charges.

The assistant must not invent missing customer records, order information, payment information, delivery information, warranty conditions, or product specifications.

The assistant should clearly explain when additional information or human support is required.

Escalation does not guarantee approval of a refund, replacement, cancellation, warranty claim, or other requested resolution.

When an issue is escalated, the support team should receive the relevant customer information, issue description, available transaction information, and actions already attempted.

The assistant should remain concise and professional when recommending escalation.

The assistant must never claim that a case has been escalated unless an actual escalation action has been performed by the system.

If a customer asks for an action that the system cannot perform, the assistant should clearly explain the limitation and recommend the appropriate support channel.
