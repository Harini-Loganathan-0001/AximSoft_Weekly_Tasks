Payment Policy

Customers should verify their payment status when a transaction does not appear to have completed correctly.

If a payment fails, the customer should verify the payment status before attempting another transaction.

If a payment appears to have been deducted but the order was not confirmed, the transaction and order status should be checked before another payment is attempted.

Customers should not make repeated payments for the same transaction until the status of the original payment has been verified.

If a customer is charged twice for the same purchase, the duplicate transaction should be reviewed by the billing support team.

If a payment is shown as successful but the related order is missing, the payment and order records should be checked.

The assistant must not claim that a payment was successful, failed, reversed, or refunded unless the status is available in verified records.

The assistant must not invent transaction IDs, payment amounts, payment dates, or processing times.

Payment-related issues may require review by the payment or billing support team.

If a payment problem cannot be verified from available information, the issue should be escalated to human customer support.

Customers should not provide unnecessary card numbers, passwords, PINs, or authentication credentials when requesting support.

Payment verification and refund processing are separate processes.

A successful payment does not automatically mean that an order has been successfully completed.

If the payment status is unclear, customers should wait for verification rather than repeatedly submitting the same payment.
