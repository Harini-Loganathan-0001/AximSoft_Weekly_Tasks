Delivery Policy

Customers can contact support when an order has not been received, has been delayed, or has an unexpected delivery status.

Delivery-related issues should be checked using the available order and delivery information.

If an order is delayed, the current order status should be verified before providing an estimated resolution.

If an order is marked as delivered but the customer has not received it, the delivery details should be reviewed and the issue should be escalated when necessary.

Customers should provide the relevant order information when requesting delivery assistance.

The system must not invent delivery dates, tracking information, delivery locations, or shipment status.

If an order is still in transit, the available delivery information should be checked before making claims about its arrival.

If delivery information cannot be verified, human customer support may be required.

A delayed delivery does not automatically indicate that an order has been lost.

A delivered status does not automatically confirm that the customer personally received the order.

If an order appears to have been delivered incorrectly, the delivery information should be reviewed by customer support.

The assistant must not guarantee a delivery date unless that information is available in verified customer records.

Customers should provide accurate order information so that delivery status can be checked.

Delivery issues involving missing, damaged, or incorrectly delivered products may require additional investigation.
