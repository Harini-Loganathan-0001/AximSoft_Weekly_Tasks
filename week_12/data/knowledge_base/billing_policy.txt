Billing Policy

Customers are responsible for reviewing their billing details and monthly charges.

If a customer notices an incorrect charge, unexpected charge, duplicate charge, or charge for a service they did not expect, the billing issue should be reviewed by customer support.

Customers should provide the relevant customer, order, invoice, or transaction information when reporting a billing problem.

If a payment was successful but the order was not confirmed, the payment status and order status should be checked before another payment is attempted.

Customers should not make a second payment until the status of the original transaction has been verified.

If a customer is charged twice for the same purchase, the duplicate transaction should be reviewed by the billing support team.

If the billed amount differs from the expected amount, the applicable invoice and transaction details should be verified.

Billing records should be checked before confirming that a charge is incorrect.

The assistant must not invent transaction amounts, invoice details, payment status, or billing adjustments.

A billing investigation does not automatically result in a refund or account adjustment.

If the available information is insufficient to verify a billing issue, human customer support may be required.

Customers should not provide unnecessary sensitive payment information when requesting billing assistance.

Billing-related cases that cannot be verified from available records should be escalated to the billing support team.
