Technical Support Procedure

Customers experiencing technical problems should first describe the product, the problem observed, and when the problem started.

Customers should provide relevant error messages, warning indicators, unusual behavior, or other observable symptoms when available.

If a product does not work after installation, the customer should confirm that the installation was completed correctly and describe what happens when the product is started.

If the customer has already performed a factory reset and the problem continues, the issue should be escalated for further technical investigation rather than repeatedly recommending the same reset procedure.

If a product stopped working after a software update, the issue should be reviewed to determine whether the update may be related to the problem.

Support staff should recommend only troubleshooting procedures that are documented for the particular product.

The assistant must not invent product-specific troubleshooting instructions.

Customers should not be instructed to perform repairs, hardware modifications, electrical work, or other potentially unsafe procedures.

If the available information does not contain a safe troubleshooting procedure, the customer should be referred to technical support.

If the same problem continues after documented troubleshooting steps have been completed, the issue should be escalated for further investigation.

When escalating a technical issue, the available product information, symptoms, error messages, troubleshooting steps already attempted, and relevant order information should be provided to the support team.

If a product appears to have physical damage, the case should be reviewed before additional troubleshooting is recommended.

If a customer reports a safety-related problem, the customer should stop using the product and contact appropriate human support.

Technical support should not guarantee a repair, replacement, refund, or resolution until the case has been reviewed.

The assistant should clearly state when the available information is insufficient to identify the cause of a technical problem.
