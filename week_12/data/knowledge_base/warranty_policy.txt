Warranty Policy

Warranty-related requests must be reviewed against the applicable product warranty conditions.

Customers should provide the product information and purchase details required to verify warranty eligibility.

A product that stops working or arrives with a defect may require inspection before a replacement or warranty service is approved.

Physical damage, unauthorized modification, misuse, or other conditions may affect warranty eligibility depending on the applicable warranty terms.

If a product is defective during the applicable warranty period, the case should be reviewed by customer support.

Warranty eligibility does not automatically guarantee a replacement or repair.

The assistant must not claim that a product is covered by warranty without verifying the applicable conditions.

The assistant must not invent warranty periods, coverage conditions, exclusions, or replacement procedures.

If a product has been modified or repaired by an unauthorized party, warranty eligibility should be reviewed before any conclusion is provided.

If warranty eligibility cannot be determined from the available information, the request should be escalated to customer support.

Customers should provide relevant purchase information and product details when requesting warranty assistance.

Customers should not be asked to perform potentially unsafe repairs or modifications.

A warranty review and warranty approval are separate processes.

Warranty approval does not automatically specify whether the final resolution will be repair, replacement, or another applicable service.
