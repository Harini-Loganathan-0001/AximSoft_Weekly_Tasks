Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund when an eligible purchase requires cancellation or when a product or service qualifies for a refund.

Refund requests must be reviewed against the applicable purchase and refund conditions.

Customers should provide the order or transaction information required to identify the purchase.

A refund request does not guarantee automatic approval.

The support team must verify the transaction and applicable refund conditions before confirming eligibility.

If a customer received a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, the case should be reviewed to determine whether a refund or replacement is applicable.

If a payment was completed but the purchase was not successfully processed, the transaction should be verified before a refund is promised.

If a refund has been approved, processing may require additional time depending on the payment method and applicable processing procedures.

The assistant must not guarantee a refund before eligibility has been verified.

The assistant must not invent refund amounts, processing times, or eligibility conditions that are not documented.

If the available information is insufficient to determine refund eligibility, the request should be escalated to human customer support.

Customers should retain relevant order or transaction information when requesting a refund.

A refund request and refund approval are separate processes. Submitting a request does not mean that the refund has already been approved.
